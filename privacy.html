Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WorkZivo may collect, use and handle information when you use this website.

WorkZivo uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may collect browser, device, approximate location, page and general usage information.

Third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. WorkZivo may display advertisements in the future through advertising partners such as Google AdSense.

Third-Party Services

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We aim to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information, but no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.
