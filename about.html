About WorkZivo

WorkZivo is a free everyday tools website designed to make common daily tasks faster and easier.

Our goal is to provide simple and useful tools for money planning, shopping, home management, travel, productivity and basic fitness calculations.

What You Can Use WorkZivo For

WorkZivo includes tools such as budget planning, expense tracking, bill splitting, fuel cost calculations, grocery planning, travel budgeting, work hour calculations, timers and other everyday utilities.

Simple and Easy to Use

Our tools work directly in your browser without requiring an account. Some tools may store information locally on your device so you can continue using them later.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build practical online utilities that save time and help users complete everyday calculations and tasks without unnecessary complexity.

Important Note

WorkZivo tools are provided for general informational and utility purposes. Results may be estimates and should not be treated as professional financial, medical, legal or other specialist advice.
